Client-facing decision framework

Recommended presentation use

This scorecard is designed to show that the relocation process is disciplined, not subjective. It helps a tenant compare competing buildings across total cost, operational fit, labor, transportation access, timing, landlord flexibility, expansion potential and qualitative business impact.

Recommended workflow:

  1. Customize criteria and weights for the client’s business priorities.
  2. Add each building with address, core specs and 1 to 5 ratings.
  3. Use the map to show realistic relocation geography and market coverage.
  4. Use the ranked list to guide discussion, not to imply the highest score is automatically the final decision.
  5. Export the client file as JSON so the same presentation can be reused and updated later.

Best format: reusable HTML dashboard, supported by optional static PowerPoint screenshots or exported tables for final client reporting.
